GLACIATION was a Horizon Europe Research and Innovation Action that developed
energy-efficient, privacy-preserving data operations across the
edge–core–cloud continuum. The project combined a distributed knowledge graph,
AI-assisted placement, and a privacy- and trust-aware metadata framework to
reduce data movement and the energy consumed by data processing.

The consortium comprised the Italian Ministry of Economy and Finance
(coordinator), Dell Technologies, HIRO Microdatacenters, Leibniz University
Hannover, the Lisbon Council, the University of Milan, the University of
Bergamo, ERCIM, EURECOM, SAP, University College Cork, Sogei, Lakeside Labs,
Engineering Ingegneria Informatica, ETH Zurich, and the Independent Power
Transmission Operator. The University of Bergamo led the work package on
protection techniques.

## Research and Results

GLACIATION addressed the environmental and privacy costs of moving and
processing large volumes of data across heterogeneous infrastructures. Its
work included:

- a distributed knowledge graph and metadata model spanning edge, core, and
  cloud resources;
- a secure, AI-enabled placement engine that selected where data and analytics
  should run while minimising data movement;
- power-measurement and energy-efficient data-management techniques for
  scheduling, caching, and task execution;
- policy models, sanitisation methods, and other protection techniques that
  incorporated privacy and trust into distributed data operations;
- an integrated platform evaluated in public-service, manufacturing, energy,
  and enterprise-data-analytics scenarios.

The complete platform architecture and its implementations are available in
the [GLACIATION GitHub organisation](https://github.com/glaciation-heu). The
[IceStream repository](https://github.com/glaciation-heu/IceStream) documents
the platform components, while the
[GitOps deployments repository](https://github.com/glaciation-heu/gitops-deployments)
provides the configuration for automated deployment of the GLACIATION platform
to Kubernetes clusters.

## Deliverables

The European Commission catalogues 26 public GLACIATION deliverables: seven
software or platform results and 19 documents and reports. They cover:

- the architecture, GLACIATION kernel, distributed-knowledge-graph services,
  and final integrated platform;
- intermediate and final releases of the secure AI-enabled placement engine;
- power measurement, energy-efficient data management, dynamic power
  allocation, and workload analysis;
- the reference metadata model, standard queries, privacy assessment, and data
  protection tools;
- use-case integration, validation, demonstration, communication, and
  exploitation.

The complete catalog and downloadable material are available on the
[CORDIS results page](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101070141/results).
The [project deliverables page](https://glaciation-project.eu/outcomes/public-deliverables)
provides the project's own grouping of the public outputs, while the
[periodic report](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101070141/reporting)
summarises the project's objectives and progress.
